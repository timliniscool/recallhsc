How RecallHSC works

  1. Pick an essay up top, or + Upload your own.
  2. Type the highlighted bit. Each sentence builds up a few words at a time.
  3. Every so often you’ll type the paragraph or the whole essay so far from memory.
  4. Spelling and punctuation don’t count. Check the marking, then Enter to keep going.
  5. Hold CtrlP to glance at the paragraph, CtrlO for the whole essay. CtrlZ on an empty box steps back.

It all saves in this browser. Rubrics and exam tips are in the Rubric guide tab.
