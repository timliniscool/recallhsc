How RecallHSC works

  1. Pick an essay from the top bar, or + Upload your own. Paragraphs, topic sentences and quotes are found automatically.
  2. Type each chunk you see. It hides as you type, then grows a chunk at a time until the sentence is done.
  3. You’ll then recall the paragraph so far, and the whole essay at each topic sentence and quote + analysis.
  4. Spelling and punctuation don’t matter. Miss too much? Read the corrections, then Enter retry or Tab skip.

Everything saves in this browser. The Rubric guide tab has module stats and exam tips.
