Privacy policy

Last updated 28 November 2026

  • Your essays stay on your device unless you sign in and turn on cloud sync.
  • No cookies, no ads, no selling data. Usage stats are anonymous and can be turned off.
  • Handwriting is read on your iPad. In the beta, signed-in users also share writing samples to improve the reader, unless they turn it off.
  • You can delete everything stored online from Settings at any time.

Who we are

RecallHSC is a free, open-source study tool for memorising HSC English essays, made by a student. The code is public on GitHub, so you can check exactly what it does. Questions or requests: open an issue on GitHub.

What's stored on your device

Your essays, progress, statistics and settings are saved in your browser's local storage on this device. They aren't sent anywhere unless you use cloud sync. Clearing your browser's site data deletes them, so use Essays → Back up if you want a copy.

Cloud sync (optional)

If you sign in (with an email link, no password) and turn on sync, these are stored in our database (Supabase) so they can follow you to other devices:

  • your email address (for signing in)
  • your essays, progress, statistics and settings

Only your account can read your data: the database enforces this for every row. You can stop syncing, delete your online data, or delete your account in Settings → Cloud sync. Deleting your account removes everything stored online. Essays on your device are kept.

Handwriting (beta, iPad with Apple Pencil)

The handwriting reader runs entirely on your iPad. Its model is downloaded once from Hugging Face and then works offline. Reading your writing doesn't send it anywhere.

Writing samples (beta only). To make the reader better at real handwriting, the beta collects samples from signed-in users. This is on by default and you can turn it off at any time in Settings → App → Share handwriting samples. For each check, a sample contains:

Samples are private: only the RecallHSC developer can see them, and they're used only to test and improve handwriting reading. They're deleted if you delete your account. To have your samples removed without deleting your account, open an issue on GitHub.

Anonymous usage statistics

We count page visits and which features are used (for example "upload" or "game mode") with GoatCounter. It uses no cookies and stores nothing on your device. It never receives your essays or anything you type or write. You can turn it off in Settings → App → Anonymous usage stats. It's also off if your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control.

Services the site loads from

  • GitHub Pages hosts the site. Like any web host, it may log basic request details such as IP addresses.
  • Google Fonts provides the typefaces.
  • Supabase stores cloud sync data (only if you sign in).
  • Hugging Face serves the handwriting model download (beta, iPad only).
  • GoatCounter counts anonymous visits (unless turned off).

Students under 18

RecallHSC is made for high school students. Everything works without an account. If you're under 18, check with a parent or guardian before signing in to cloud sync.
